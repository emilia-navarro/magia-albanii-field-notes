Magia Albanii — Field Notes
===========================

Magia Albanii Field Notes is a documentation space for organizing source lists, field notes, editorial structures and cultural references connected with Albania and the Western Balkans.

The project focuses on practical travel writing, cultural landscapes, regional folklore, heritage context, route planning and photography-based field documentation.

Main areas
----------

- Albania and the Western Balkans
- Albanian Alps / Prokletije
- Lake Koman and Shala River
- Albanian Riviera
- Cultural landscapes and local heritage
- Oral stories, regional folklore and beliefs
- Practical route notes and travel planning
- Visual field documentation

Author
------

Emilia Navarro is a travel writer, content editor and visual note-taker based in Tirana, Albania. Her work focuses on research-driven regional guides, cultural storytelling, Balkan heritage, local stories and practical travel documentation.
